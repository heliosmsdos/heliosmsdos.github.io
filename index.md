## ❓ Troubleshooting

**The device is not detected.** Unplug and replug the USB cable, then check that USB debugging is still enabled. Try a different cable — charge-only cables are a surprisingly common cause.

**The app opens but nothing happens.** Some apps need a moment to load. Increase the wait time in the settings so the automation pauses before acting.

**A task stops partway through.** This usually means the phone locked itself or a pop-up appeared. Unlock the phone and restart the task.

**Messages are not sending.** Confirm the recipient list is in the expected format and that the account is logged in on the device.
